Coffee Drinks

Explore the most common types of coffee drinks and what makes each one special.

Espresso

Rich and intense. The base for most coffee drinks.

Americano

Espresso with hot water. Smooth and bold.

Latte

Espresso with steamed milk. Creamy and mild.

Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, milk, and foam. Balanced and warm.

Mocha

Chocolate, espresso, and milk. A sweet treat.

Flat White

Smooth microfoam and rich espresso flavor.

Coffee Beans

Learn about the different types of coffee beans and where they come from.

Arabica

Sweet, smooth, and often floral or fruity. The most popular bean.

Robusta

Strong and bold with higher caffeine. Earthy flavor profile.

Liberica

Unique smoky and woody flavors. Less common but memorable.

Roast Levels

Roasting affects flavor, aroma, and strength. Explore the three main roast styles.

Light Roast

Bright and fruity with higher acidity.

Medium Roast

Balanced flavor and aroma. A crowd favorite.

Dark Roast

Bold, smoky, and intense with low acidity.
